Privacy Policy — Pocket Voice AI

Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. Your Data Stays On Your Device

Pocket Voice AI processes all notifications and voice commands locally on your phone. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data. There are no servers and no cloud.

2. Notification Access

The app uses Android's NotificationListenerService only to read your notifications aloud and enable voice replies. This permission is required for core functionality. Your notification content is never sent anywhere.

3. Microphone

The microphone is used only for voice commands and voice replies, processed on-device using your phone's speech recognition. Audio is not recorded or uploaded.

4. Permissions Summary

  • Notification Access: read notifications aloud
  • Microphone: voice commands & replies
  • Post Notifications: show app alerts
  • Battery Optimization: keep the service running

5. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that collect personal data. The app works fully offline.

6. Data Deletion

All history and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently.

7. Contact

For questions, contact the developer via the Play Store listing.
